# Sound effects

The local sound-effect set was generated on 2026-08-06 with ElevenLabs Sound
Effects (`eleven_text_to_sound_v2`), then trimmed and converted to mono 44.1 kHz
16-bit WAV files for Godot Web.

Included local sounds:

- Robot: jump, land, alternating footsteps, death, and respawn
- Gameplay: coin collect, enemy stomp, checkpoint raise, goal flag lower, and
  level complete
- Interface: hover, confirm, back/cancel, and pause/resume

## License boundary

These takes were generated while the account was on the ElevenLabs Free plan.
They are for non-commercial use only and public use requires attribution to
`elevenlabs.io` or `11.ai`. They must be embedded in the game and must not be
distributed as standalone audio files or as a sound library.

For that reason, `assets/audio/sfx/*.wav` is intentionally ignored by Git. A
commercial build must replace these takes with sounds generated while a paid
commercial license is active, or with independently licensed assets.

Before publishing a Free-plan build, keep the itch.io project strictly free and
non-commercial, add `Sound effects generated with elevenlabs.io` to the public
title/attribution, and do not enable donations, pay-what-you-want, ads, IAP, or
sponsorship.
